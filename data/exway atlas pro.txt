Model: Exway atlas pro

Performance & Power 
Motor Options: Available in 2WD (dual motor) or 4WD (quad motor) configurations.
Top Speed: Up to 60 km/h (37 mph).
Max Range: Around 50-70 km (31-43 miles), depending on battery size and riding conditions.
Hill Climbing: Handles 35-40% inclines with ease.
Torque: High torque for off-road and high-speed riding.

Battery & Charging 
Battery Options: Standard and larger battery options available.
Swappable Battery: Users can change batteries for extended range.
Charging Time: Fast charger available to reduce charge time significantly.

Build & Design 
Deck Material: High-quality carbon fiber or composite materials.
Wheel Options: All-terrain pneumatic wheels for off-road capability.
Weight: Heavier than standard e-skateboards, especially in 4WD mode.
Water Resistance: Some level of water resistance, but not fully waterproof.

Control & Features 
Remote Control: Smart Bluetooth remote with real-time stats.
App Integration: Fully customizable riding modes via the Exway App.
Drive System: Belt-driven motors for better torque and hill climbing.

Positive Reviews 
Powerful & Fast: Riders praise its high top speed and acceleration, making it one of the most powerful e-skateboards.
All-Terrain Performance: Handles rough roads, grass, and dirt trails well, thanks to its big pneumatic wheels.
Solid Build Quality: Durable materials and premium design make it feel like a high-end board.
Swappable Battery & Motors: The ability to switch between 4WD and 2WD is a favorite feature.
Smart App Integration: Riders like the customization options and ride-tracking via the Exway app.

Negative Reviews 
Expensive: It’s on the higher end of the price range for e-skateboards.
Heavy: Some users mention it’s difficult to carry, especially in 4WD mode.
Battery Life Varies: Depending on terrain and rider weight, the range may be lower than advertised.
Remote Connection Issues: A few users report occasional disconnections with the remote.
